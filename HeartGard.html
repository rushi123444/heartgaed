Understanding Heart Disease

Learn about cardiovascular health, risk factors, and how to maintain a healthy heart.

What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease refers to several types of heart conditions, with coronary artery disease being the most common.

  • Plaque buildup in arteries
  • Reduced blood flow to heart
  • Can lead to heart attack

Major Risk Factors

Several factors increase your risk of developing heart disease.

  • High blood pressure
  • High cholesterol
  • Smoking
  • Diabetes
  • Family history

Warning Signs

Recognize these symptoms and seek immediate medical attention.

  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Pain in arms, back, neck
  • Nausea or lightheadedness

Prevention Strategies

Simple lifestyle changes can significantly reduce your risk of heart disease.

Healthy Diet

Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins.

5+ servings of fruits/vegetables daily
Omega-3 rich foods twice weekly
Limit saturated fats and sodium

Regular Exercise

Stay active with at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise weekly.

30 minutes daily walking
Strength training 2x per week
Cardiovascular activities

Weight Management

Maintain a healthy BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 to reduce heart disease risk.

Monitor BMI regularly
Waist circumference < 40" (men)
Gradual weight loss if overweight

Quit Smoking

Smoking is a major risk factor. Quitting can immediately benefit your heart health.

Benefits start within 20 minutes
Lung function improves in weeks
Heart attack risk drops 50% in 1 year

Quality Sleep

Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night for optimal heart health.

Consistent sleep schedule
No screens before bed
Dark, quiet sleep environment

Stress Management

Chronic stress can damage your heart. Practice relaxation techniques daily.

Meditation or yoga
Social connections
Regular leisure activities

Emergency Information

Know the signs of a heart attack and act immediately. Every minute counts.

Heart Attack Symptoms

  • Chest discomfort - pressure, squeezing, fullness
  • Pain or discomfort in arms, back, neck, jaw, stomach
  • Shortness of breath with or without chest discomfort
  • Breaking out in a cold sweat, nausea, lightheadedness

What to Do

Call 911 Immediately
Don't wait more than 5 minutes
Chew Aspirin
If not allergic, chew one 325mg tablet
